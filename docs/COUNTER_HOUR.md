## What this hour taught us

1. **Relatives bring the phone** — the phone's mobile number becomes the applicant's contact, often without the elder's awareness.
2. **The one-per-mobile rule surprises everyone** — CSC operators, relatives helping two family members, and people who think the rule is about the *applicant*, not the *phone*.
3. **Passbook data is trusted but wrong** — IFSC typos, old branch codes, spaces and hyphens in account numbers are the norm.
4. **Nobody knows the exact birth date** — "born the year of the big flood" is real. Documents often disagree; the assistant must ask for what is *printed*, not what the person guesses.
5. **The window confuses people** — the difference between "apply now" and "you are eligible now" is lost on most people. Under-60s who turn 60 before 31 Oct are the hard case.
6. **Sign-out is not a habit** — kiosks and shared phones mean the next user is one tap away from the previous person's session.
7. **Already-approved people come back** — disbursement delays look like application problems.
8. **Languages mix** — Assamese script names, Hindi numerals, English form fields, and Romanised Assamese all appear in the same application.
